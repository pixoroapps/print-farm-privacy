Privacy Policy

Idle 3D Print Farm · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixoro Apps ("we", "us") handles information when you play the mobile game Idle 3D Print Farm (the "Game"). We designed the Game to collect as little information as possible. The Game has no user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, printers, contracts, settings such as sound and vibration) is saved only on your device. We do not upload your save file to our servers. Deleting the Game or its data removes this progress.

2. Information collected automatically

To keep the Game working and to improve it, the Game uses the following third-party services:

ServicePurposeData
GameAnalytics Anonymous gameplay analytics: which features are used, where players get stuck, game balance and stability. Gameplay events (e.g. tutorial steps, workshop level reached, in-game coins earned and spent, completed contracts, rewarded video views, time away from the game), session start/end and length, device model and manufacturer, operating system version, app version, language, a random installation identifier and, where available, the device's advertising identifier. Your IP address is used to determine an approximate country and is not stored by us.
Google Firebase (Installations, Remote Config) Delivering game configuration (for example, telling an outdated version that an update is available). A random Firebase installation identifier, app identifier and version, platform. Your IP address is processed by Google to deliver the response.
Google AdMob Showing rewarded video ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud and, with your consent where required, personalizing ads. Advertising identifier, device and app information, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (views, clicks), diagnostics. See how Google uses information for advertising.
Google Play Distributing and updating the Game, crash and performance reports. Handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

We do not receive information that identifies you personally. We use analytics data only in aggregate to operate, analyze and improve the Game. We do not sell your information.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, mainly optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game bonuses. AdMob collects the data listed above to serve and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the Game asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before ads are personalized or data is used for that purpose; you can change your choice later in the Game's settings. Without consent you may still see non-personalized ads.

4. Purchases

The Game does not contain in-app purchases. All in-game currency is virtual and has no real-world value.

5. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

We process analytics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating, securing and improving the Game, and use data for personalized advertising only with your consent. You may object to processing or withdraw consent at any time in the Game's settings, by contacting us or by uninstalling the Game.

6. Data retention

Analytics and advertising data is retained by GameAnalytics, Firebase and Google AdMob according to their retention policies and is kept no longer than needed for the purposes above. Data stored on your device remains until you delete the Game or its data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset or delete your device's advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads), and opt out of personalized ads there or at adssettings.google.com.
  • Uninstalling the Game stops all data collection by the Game.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data. Because we do not collect data that identifies you directly, please include the identifiers you want us to look for, or contact the services listed above. Write to pixoro.apps@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads in the Game are not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All network communication of the Game uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States and the European Union, with appropriate safeguards required by law.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Continuing to use the Game after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
