Data deletion request

Idle 3D Print Farm by Pixoro Apps

Idle 3D Print Farm has no user accounts. Your game progress is stored only on your device, and the analytics and advertising data we use is linked to random device identifiers, not to your name or email.

Delete game progress

Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Print Farm → Storage → Clear storage.

Request deletion of analytics data

  1. Send an email to pixoro.apps@gmail.com with the subject “Idle 3D Print Farm data deletion”.
  2. If possible, include your device model and the approximate date you last played, so we can find the matching records in GameAnalytics.
  3. We will delete the matching analytics data (gameplay events, device information, installation identifiers) within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept after deletion.

Advertising data

Ad data is processed by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and manage Google ad data at myadcenter.google.com.

Українською

Щоб видалити аналітичні дані, напишіть на pixoro.apps@gmail.com з темою «Idle 3D Print Farm data deletion», вказавши модель пристрою й приблизну дату останньої гри. Ми видалимо дані протягом 30 днів і підтвердимо листом.
